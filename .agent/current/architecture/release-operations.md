### Release Update and Promotion Contract

- Every activation pins one runtime build id, app-shell version, manifest
  schema, and asset-catalog root. A complete non-revoked build may finish its
  run. Updates stage separately and take control only at the magazine after all
  activation transactions are terminal and the target migrations, restore, and
  complete content preflight succeed. No runtime or catalog component hot-swaps
  during preparation or play.
- Only a signed monotonic versioned release policy from a pinned trust root can
  revoke exact build, manifest, migration, or binding ids and hashes. It names
  reason, effective window, minimum compatible build, migration path, and
  expiry. A cached verified policy authorizes offline use only within its
  validity; unavailable required freshness yields `Connect to verify`, and
  revocation preserves unrelated content and all durable player state.
- The last-known-good policy-allowed release, its leases, and the original save
  remain immutable. A target build, catalog, content, and copy-on-write
  migrated save stage under separate ids and prove complete restore before one
  atomic active-pointer swap. Failure discards staging. A revoked prior release
  is not silently reactivated.
- Compatible noncritical updates adopt at the magazine with an accessible
  `Updated` notice and details foldout. `Review Update` becomes the sole hero
  only when migration needs a player choice. `Later` exists only while the
  current release remains policy-supported. Signed critical revocation uses
  state-preserving `Update and Return`.
- CI creates an immutable candidate identified by build, shell, catalog,
  dependency, and provenance hashes. Signed promotion requires build/route and
  schema gates, deterministic simulation, copy-on-write migration and rollback
  rehearsal, browser human-view, public phone, lifecycle-recovery, performance,
  and O5 acceptance bound to the exact candidate. A branch, `dist` directory,
  deployment, or notification grants no release authority.

### Privacy-Preserving Diagnostics and Operational Recovery

- Detailed diagnostic records remain local by default. A fail-closed schema
  may export only explicitly consented coarse build, typed failure, approved
  device-tier, and bucketed timing fields without persistent identity. Camera,
  microphone, room geometry, precise location, raw gestures, saves,
  discoveries, URLs, and raw logs are excluded. `Share Diagnostics` must show
  the exact fields, purpose, destination, retention, and deletion path before
  send.
- Diagnostic consent is independent of camera access, terms, and play, defaults
  off, and is requested just in time for one reviewed package or bounded
  session. Decline preserves full play. Withdrawal deletes unsent data, and
  shared-device reset expires both consent and pending records.
- Predeclared build-bound failure, crash-loop, migration, and performance
  thresholds include minimum sample, coverage, window, and confidence rules
  over allowlisted aggregates. A breach freezes further magazine-boundary
  adoption and creates immutable O5 evidence; it cannot replace signed release
  policy as rollback or revocation authority.
- Each exact build journals a boot attempt and later healthy marker. Three
  consecutive pre-healthy failures in a bounded window route to a minimal
  pinned recovery shell without AR or candidate code. The shell preserves saves
  and verified cache and exposes only policy-supported restore,
  state-preserving update, optional previewed diagnostics, and a confirmed
  advanced reset.
- Local diagnostic storage is a byte-and-age-bounded ring. Submitted packages
  have purpose-specific retention and receipts; primary and backup copies
  expire on declared schedules, deletion works by receipt, only thresholded
  non-reidentifiable aggregates may remain, deletion is auditable, and no
  diagnostic lifecycle action mutates gameplay state.
- An approved support package is assembled and canonically hashed on-device,
  encrypted to a pinned first-party key, and sent only to an authenticated
  signed-policy endpoint with nonce, expiry, and idempotency. A signed receipt
  carries the retention deadline and deletion token; a failed package remains
  local only for its bounded TTL and cannot expand the previewed consent.
- Submitted records are encrypted at rest and available only to trained,
  case-assigned support under purpose-bound expiring grants. Every read, export,
  and deletion is immutably audited. Bulk browsing and unrelated reuse are
  forbidden; dual-approved alerted break-glass is the only exceptional path,
  and only thresholded non-reidentifiable aggregates cross the case boundary.
- Every diagnostic schema has a canonical version and hash bound to the exact
  build and signed release policy. CI and runtime reject unknown fields, and
  preview exposes a human-readable diff. Prior consent applies only to a
  strictly narrower compatible schema with unchanged purpose, destination,
  retention, and deletion; any expansion requires renewed just-in-time consent,
  while legacy decoding and receipt deletion survive the support window.
- Before sharing consent, the device records only an essential transparent
  ring of exact build, boot/healthy markers, privacy-safe typed failure,
  approved tier, and bucketed timing. Any approved expanded local-debug mode
  requires separate temporary consent. Prohibited fields remain excluded in
  every mode, storage and deletion controls stay visible, and export consent is
  independent.
- A confirmed compromise uses signed policy to disable and quarantine only
  affected endpoints, keys, schemas, and package windows, rotates trust, and
  preserves immutable evidence. Receipt-scoped status, deletion, and
  guardian-facing notice require no persistent identity. Gameplay state remains
  intact, and submission resumes only after security review and O5 approval of
  exact replacement artifacts.
