## Validation Ladder

Batch 056 provisionally treats all eight gates below as conjunctive and
non-substitutable while leaving direct user override open. Every verdict must
bind the same build, shell, catalog, recipe, asset, calibration, frame, policy,
and graph hashes; O5 must mark the cited evidence applicable; unresolved
`R3/R4` risks fail; and `R2` passes only when that exact gate manifest declares
its owner, mitigation, expiry, and ceiling. Any relevant hash change makes
dependent proof stale and requires the affected gates to run again.

The eight surfaces have distinct authority:

- contract/static proves declared structure, ownership, provenance, complete
  bindings, accessibility contracts, and absence of production placeholders;
- deterministic headless proof establishes semantic replay, boundary, save,
  migration, rollback, corruption, completion, and reward invariants;
- the spatial simulator establishes exact fixture behavior but never physical
  sensing, contact, comfort, reach, or safety;
- production build and route proof establishes the exact deployable bytes and
  every required entry path;
- browser plus O5 human view establishes interaction, hierarchy,
  comprehensibility, accessibility, and visual continuity;
- a public phone establishes HTTPS entry, permission, camera, viewport,
  lifecycle, storage, network, history, resume, and completion behavior;
- physical AR establishes real sensing, placement, contact, viewing, reach,
  tracking, recovery, comfort, and safety on the accepted device/session
  matrix;
- performance establishes all supported-class budgets without semantic
  degradation.

The recommended physical gate inherits Question 266's six devices across three
capability/performance classes and twelve mixed-height sessions, including four
seated and four one-handed sessions. It also inherits Question 276's ten
tracking-loss cycles on each device and requires zero unsafe confirmations,
unpaused safety events, false resumes, or post-pause gameplay ticks. This
contract remains unapproved and unimplemented.
