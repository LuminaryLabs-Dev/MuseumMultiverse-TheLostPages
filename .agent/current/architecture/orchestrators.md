### O2. Spatial Frame Director

- Primary skill: `spatial-frame-orchestrator` — proposed
- Role: own normalized wall, tabletop, and floor host adapters, with guided wall
  placement and the stable frame-local coordinate system for Page 05.
- Mission: convert each accepted physical surface—and Page 05's selected frame
  asset—into a safe, adjustable, locked canonical placement descriptor.
- Owns: surface selection, pose proposal, player adjustment, orientation, scale,
  pivot, bounds, anchors, contact, fit, clearance, floor-reference recovery,
  story-native all-eight surface map, gallery-wall/tabletop/floor-pedestal
  physical families, per-route support/contact/depth/safe-zone/readability
  profiles, checkpoint-safe cross-family re-placement,
  typed module-socket spatial validation inside the locked frame, standard and
  seated viewing-zone descriptors, capability-aware obstruction evidence,
  tracking-loss lifecycle, same-anchor recovery, re-placement handoff, and
  placement evidence.
- Does not own: platformer rules, rewards, materials, or renderer object
  references.
- Delegates to: M1 and A1-A7.
- Output: one cross-experience normalized anchor descriptor plus serializable
  surface-map and scale-family catalogs; wall, tabletop, floor/pedestal, frame,
  floor-reference, viewing-zone, lane-anchor, and typed
  module-socket descriptors, safe envelope, adjustment limits, locked
  transform, module-fit results, and numeric plus visible evidence.
- Memory emphasis: recurring coordinate conversions, device-specific placement
  failures, trusted wall-fit patterns, and known weak surfaces.
- Validation rule: finite transforms, normalized axes, stable pivot, wall
  contact within tolerance, a plane within 5 degrees of vertical, a frame
  center adjustable within 1.20-1.50 meters above the detected floor, a
  physically proven range around the provisional 1.0-1.5 meter width target,
  15% clear wall on every side, readable viewing from 1.5-2.5 meters and through
  30 degrees off-axis, a visible and player-confirmed 1.5-by-1.0-meter standard
  viewing zone, a tested seated preset and stationary zone, no unsafe overflow,
  and visible player confirmation. A missing floor triggers bounded guided
  floor scanning. Sensing assists when supported but is never silently claimed.
  A detected or reported live obstruction freezes play until re-confirmation.
  Tracking loss freezes play; provisional same-anchor
  recovery is bounded to five seconds with `Re-place Frame` always available.
  Resume permits at most 3% frame-width translation and 3 degrees of rotation,
  then eases to the stored pose while frozen. Failed recovery routes to
  re-placement with checkpoint preservation. Rejection routes to the labeled
  tabletop fallback. A cross-family change closes the old host placement and
  resumes only after complete new-family preflight, immutable pinning, and
  player confirmation at an activation/checkpoint/recovery boundary.
- Recommended next skill: O3 after the frame pose is locked.
