## Three Checkpointed Sections

### Section 1: Learn Jump

- Introduce JR, movement timing, one lane, readable hazards, and safe recovery.
- Begin in charcoal shadows with Page 02 teal accents and small paper movements.
- JR auto-runs; teach one fixed, forgiving tap-to-Jump arc.
- Folding ledges, tearing gaps, ink hazards, Jump arcs, and tactile cues
  communicate action.
- No route manipulation is required.

### Section 2: Learn Manipulate

- Pause traversal and introduce sliding or rotating picture layers.
- Introduce a second lane and one authored ramp, bridge, or portal transition
  that JR follows automatically during Run.
- Optional physical viewpoint movement reveals lane relationships.
- Select one layer and drag it along its constrained authored track.
- Let that layer change platforms, archive-machinery behavior, and optional
  discovery access within validated bounds.
- Require one valid route lock before JR runs the section.
- Shift into Page 05 paper-olive as the archive responds to JR's planning.
- Preserve the first section's Jump knowledge without adding every challenge at
  once.

### Section 3: Combine at the Portal

- Inspect the layered scene.
- Adjust up to two picture layers and lock a valid route.
- Traverse front, middle, and back lanes with Jump while authored connections
  move JR between them automatically.
- Reach the breathing portal and complete the experience.
- Let the scene bloom into Page 08 portal gold and confident museum magic.
- Use animated relics, bounded beams, portal pulses, museum light, chimes, and
  frame motion for the climax.

Target successful duration: three to five minutes total.

## Feedback Contract

Default contract:

- visual and text feedback communicate every required state;
- paper foley and light haptics are optional capability enhancements;
- spatial audio activates only when headphone output is available and enabled;
- mute, no-vibration, no-headphone, and reduced-motion paths preserve complete
  meaning;
- the platformer emits feedback intent but never owns device playback.

Ordinary play uses living-paper identity:

- fold and layer motion;
- ink or halftone trails;
- page-snap timing cues;
- soft tactile sound;
- restrained haptic confirmation.

Milestones use museum magic:

- breathing frame;
- artifact chime;
- guided light beam;
- portal-ring expansion;
- spatial completion sound;
- stronger but optional haptic pulse.

Feedback provisionally uses one closed twelve-id catalog with complete
non-color visual/text meaning; bounded paper, confirmation, caution, and
milestone audio at `-26/-23/-21/-20 LUFS`; optional normalized
`0.12/0.20/0.28/0.35` haptic classes; `35%` default sound, `Light` haptics, and
spatial-Off unless headphones are positively reported or declared; static
reduced-motion equivalents; silent and screen-reader parity; stable-event
deduplication; cue/asset/fallback hashes; and public-space proof.
