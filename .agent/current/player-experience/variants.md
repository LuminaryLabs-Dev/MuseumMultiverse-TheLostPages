## Authored Variants and Discoveries

- Page 05 provisionally contains three complete authored course variants.
- Each variant preserves the same three-section teaching arc, target duration,
  completion condition, assistance contract, and required Page 05 reward.
- Variants rotate in a deterministic unseen-first order. Rotation advances only
  after successful portal completion; failure or abandonment retains the
  current variant.
- Routes, hazard arrangements, layer tracks, and optional discovery placements
  vary while story milestones and the approved asset library remain shared.
  Every permitted state must pass deterministic reachability, collision,
  pacing, reset, snapshot, and human-view proof.
- Picture-layer transforms may change platform connectivity, hazard behavior,
  and optional discovery access. They never change gravity, time rules, camera
  orientation, or baseline player ability.
- Each variant contains one unique archive-lore record and one unique cosmetic
  JR pose or frame embellishment.
- Optional discoveries persist immediately across failure, abandonment, replay,
  and restore.
- Discoveries never alter Jump, assistance, completion, required rewards, or
  Page 08 progression.
- After all three variants are complete, the secondary `Replay` action continues
  the fixed cycle; `Replay details` exposes an optional mastered-variant picker.
- The variants form one archive-restoration trilogy: `The Folded Stacks`
  emphasizes living-paper ledges and vertical folds; `The Misfiled Machine`
  emphasizes moving shelves, shutters, and sorting bridges; and `Frames Within
  Frames` emphasizes nested portals and three-lane transformations.
- One versioned route-local rotation record stores the ordered stable ids,
  pinned current id and recipe revision, completed set, cursor, acknowledged
  completion, and mastered-picker state. It retains incomplete runs, advances
  only on durable portal completion, migrates explicitly, cycles
  deterministically after mastery, and exposes only mastered variants in the
  picker.

  matrix enumerates at most three Section 2 and nine Section 3 combinations and
  maps each to connectivity, hazard behavior, discovery access, cues, and lock

  deterministic proof.

  contracts. Variants use curated subsets, introduce at most one unfamiliar
  archetype at a time, and activate at most two hazards simultaneously.
