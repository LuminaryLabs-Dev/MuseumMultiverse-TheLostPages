## Question 411: First Surface Pass

Status: provisionally self-answered; user override remains open

Provisional selection: 1

Why it matters: Batch 082 now provisionally requires a hash-bound silhouette
readiness packet before downstream visual work begins. The first bounded
surface node should preserve the walking bay's proven gameplay read before
ornament, mood, or microdetail consumes contrast and device budgets.

Question: Which surface work should come first?

1. Build one `Critical Readability Surface Grammar`. Apply final semantic
   material identities and non-color treatments first to canonical JR, safe
   landings and top edges, folded-ledge and tearing-gap cues, the stitched
   bridge, book-spine stair, living-paper ramp, recovery marks, and safe
   handoff. Review them under neutral authored lighting in phone, human-view,
   accessibility, performance, static, reduced-motion, silent, high-contrast,
   and fallback forms. Preserve the accepted `Handled Conservation Object`
   finish, but unlock hero-frame fragments, backing and dressing, microdetail,
   effects, and key art only after critical edge, contact, hazard, and goal
   distinctions pass without decorative support.
2. Finish the hero frame first with oak, worn gilt, oxidized brass, book cloth,
   waxed iron, rag paper, opening leaves, conservation joins, and signature
   ornament, then fit route materials and contrast into the remaining visual
   hierarchy.
3. Establish final lighting, atmosphere, parallax, particles, reactions, and
   emotional color first, then tune material identities, contact edges,
   hazards, accessibility forms, and low-tier fallback inside that mood.
4. Complete paper fibers, scratches, stains, edge wear, stitch variation,
   labels, decals, dust, and other close-up microdetail first, then derive the
   critical surface grammar from the finished asset set.

## Question 412: Walking-Bay Critical Surface Map

Status: provisionally self-answered; user override remains open

Provisional selection: 1

Why it matters: the shared safe, hazard, checkpoint, material-layer, and JR
grammars are already provisional, but the first bay mixes rag paper, book
cloth, book spines, stitching, and torn paper. Their natural material
differences need one role-locked mapping so they enrich the conservation object
without weakening the learned gameplay language.

Question: Which material-role map should govern the walking bay's critical
surfaces?

1. Use one `Role-Locked Conservation Map`. Preserve JR's ink-navy, warm-paper,
   and rust-ochre identity with a pinned light or dark outline. Give every safe
   contact one continuous high-contrast double top lip over its natural
   substrate: quiet warm rag paper for the safe landing, fold, and living-paper
   ramp; woven olive cloth for the stitched bridge; and restrained alternating
   cloth and paper bodies for the book-spine stair. Give tearing hazards a
   broken blackened-iron single edge, inward notches, and crosshatch; give
   recovery anchors the stitched-bookmark grammar; and give the safe handoff
   the safe lip plus one repaired-gilt forward tab. Bind every role to semantic
   ink, texture, silhouette, label, and authored light or dark variants, with
   independent collision and no meaning carried by color or substrate alone.
2. Use material-first naturalism. Let each paper, cloth, book, gilt, wood, and
   iron surface keep its native edge, color, wear, and light response, adding
   only small labels where gameplay roles are otherwise ambiguous.
3. Use one flat semantic recolor. Make all safe contacts green, every hazard
   red, recovery blue, and the handoff gold across every substrate, treating
   color as the primary role signal and suppressing most conservation-material
   variation.
4. Use live environment adaptation. Sample camera color, room brightness,
   reflections, and device exposure to choose each critical surface's color,
   contrast, roughness, and outline at runtime, allowing the same role to
   change materially between placements.

## Question 413: Walking-Bay Readability Light Proof

Status: provisionally self-answered; user override remains open

Provisional selection: 1

Why it matters: the current generic level declares one palette and matte paper
material but no explicit lighting proof, while the shared three-layer lighting
contract still leaves the walking bay's acceptance fixtures open. The first
surface pass needs deterministic stress evidence before atmosphere can hide a
weak role distinction.

Question: Which lighting fixture set should gate critical surface acceptance?

1. Use one `Pinned Readability Light Matrix`. Serialize an effect-free neutral
   reference key/fill, a low-ambient floor, a bright frontal wash, a hard
   raking side wash, and fixed warm/cool synthetic camera-background
   composites with exact exposure, tone, shadow, camera, and crop settings.
   Review canonical Plan and Run phone views, physical-view crops, and static,
   high-contrast, and low-tier forms. Accept only when JR, safe contacts,
   hazards, recovery, and handoff remain distinct through shape, texture,
   outline, and label in every fixture. A pinned fixture may select an authored
   light or dark variant; live room pixels and auto-exposure may never select
   semantic meaning.
2. Use one flattering neutral studio setup at the canonical desktop camera,
   accepting the surface pass when its beauty render and a single phone crop
   look clear without dark-room, bright-room, side-light, or fallback stress.
3. Use the final dramatic Page 05 atmosphere, frame-local pools, bloom,
   shadows, particles, and milestone lights as the only review environment,
   tuning critical surfaces until they read inside that completed mood.
4. Let each device sample live camera brightness and color, adjust exposure,
   material values, outlines, glow, and contrast automatically, and accept the
   pass when the adaptive presentation looks readable in the current room.

## Question 414: Walking-Bay Surface Tier Package

Status: provisionally self-answered; user override remains open

Provisional selection: 1

Why it matters: the provisional performance contract already pins C1, C2, or
C3 before activation and permits only neutral presentation to scale, but the
current generic level has one untiered matte paper material. The first bay needs
an exact surface ladder that reduces cost without changing learned meaning or
silently dropping critical art.

Question: How should the walking bay's critical surfaces degrade across device
tiers?

1. Use one `Semantic-Constant Surface Ladder`. Keep identical semantic material
   ids, masks, safe lips, hazard marks, recovery and handoff cues, silhouettes,
   labels, contact geometry, and independent collision in C1, C2, and C3. Let
   C3 retain full approved substrate response, fine normal and roughness detail,
   and bounded patina; let C2 use compressed atlases, coarser textures,
   simplified normals, and less patina; let C1 use simple opaque matte
   substrates with semantic ink, stable textures, and labels while removing
   optional patina, fine normals, reflections, and decorative shadow response.
   Keep high-contrast and static accessibility variants orthogonal to
   performance tiers. Pin one complete manifest before activation; permit a
   change only at a checkpoint with semantic parity, complete preflight, and
   rollback. A missing valid C1 package blocks reveal with labeled recovery
   rather than deleting a critical cue.
2. Author only the C3 surface package, then let texture resizing, shader
   simplification, and mesh decimation run automatically on each device,
   accepting whatever semantic masks, labels, edges, and material distinctions
   survive.
3. Begin with the complete surface treatment and progressively remove outlines,
   labels, hazard hatching, recovery marks, safe-edge geometry, and handoff
   cues at runtime whenever frame time rises, restoring them when performance
   improves.
4. Redesign each tier independently: give C3 the conservation-material system,
   C2 a simplified color-coded version, and C1 generic blocks and glow, allowing
   critical role language and palette meaning to change with device class.

## Question 415: Critical-Surface-to-Decoration Handoff

Status: provisionally self-answered; user override remains open

Provisional selection: 1

Why it matters: Questions 411-414 define the proposed first surface node,
role map, lighting proof, and tier ladder, but the current generic app still
places its one material and effect inside the same loose visual dataset.
Hero-frame finish, backing, dressing, atmosphere, microdetail, effects, and key
art need a precise consumer gate so they cannot cover weak cues or keep working
against stale critical inputs.

Question: What should unlock decorative surface development?

1. Require one hash-bound `Critical Surface Acceptance Packet`. Pin the
   accepted silhouette-readiness identity, semantic ink and substrate maps,
   role-locked surface bindings, source and adaptation hashes, reserved
   readability masks, `Pinned Readability Light Matrix` fixtures and results,
   complete C1/C2/C3 manifests, accessibility variants, cameras and crops,
   physical-view, browser and human-view, performance, fallback, risks, and
   producer/consumer verdicts. Unlock hero-frame finish, backing/dressing,
   atmosphere/motion/effects, microdetail, and key art as separate bounded
   nodes, each declaring consumed hashes, masks, and budgets. Any critical
   edge, cue, role map, source, tier, fixture, camera, or evidence change makes
   only affected nodes stale. Decoration may never enter critical masks or
   gain gameplay authority, and this gate grants no placeholder retirement,
   production promotion, push, or deployment.
2. Put the latest material files, atlases, screenshots, fixture images, and
   notes in one shared folder, then let every decorative discipline infer the
   current revision, protected masks, budgets, fallbacks, and invalidation
   scope from filenames and visual comparison.
3. Approve one polished desktop beauty render of the walking bay, then open
   every frame, dressing, lighting, detail, effect, and key-art task together
   without exact device, accessibility, fallback, performance, or hash-bound
   evidence.
4. Start all critical and decorative surface work in parallel before the role
   map, light matrix, and tier manifests pass, then repaint, reauthor, or
   manually reconcile downstream work whenever a critical dependency changes.
