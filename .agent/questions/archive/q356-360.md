## Question 359: Page 03 Motion Readability

Status: provisionally self-answered; user override remains open

Provisional selection: 1

Why it matters: `Glide`, `Lift`, and `Loop` already have deterministic paths,
dwells, and phases, but the current app exposes only moving scribbles. Players
need to predict safe arrivals without guessing from animation or relying on
color, sound, or fast reaction alone.

Question: How should Page 03 show each creature-platform's movement timing?

1. Draw one persistent faint graphite path with named start and end stamps,
   direction hatching, and fixed phase ticks for each creature. Render the live
   creature darker than its guide, leave a short bounded motion trace, place a
   small ghost impression at the next dwell, and thicken the paper saddle's
   double edge during one deterministic approach window. Match those cues with
   a concise arrival label, optional soft pencil beat, and screen-reader
   announcement derived from the authoritative phase. Pause and restore retain
   the exact phase; reduced effects remove the trace but preserve the path,
   ghost, edge, text, and timing meaning. Presentation never changes motion or
   landing eligibility.
2. Show no paths, endpoints, phase marks, approach cues, labels, or
   announcements; require players to watch several full cycles and memorize
   timing from movement alone.
3. Hide the book under full-screen camera zooms, flashing exposure, strong
   vibration, and loud beats whenever any creature approaches, using sensory
   intensity rather than stable route-local timing information.
4. Generate random trails, particles, pauses, speed changes, fake endpoints,
   and arrival effects every cycle, allowing renderer timing to decide when a
   creature appears safe to land on.
