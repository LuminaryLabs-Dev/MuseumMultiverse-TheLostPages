Status: provisionally self-answered; user override remains open

Provisional selection: 1

Why it matters: bounded skills cannot rely on chat memory, but copying every

Question: How should durable project memory and reusable learning work?

1. Keep four explicit surfaces. `.agent/` remains the human-readable discovery
   layer for goals, evidence, questions, provisional/approved decisions,
   synthesized design, and current architecture; its ledgers preserve history
   while active files stay concise. `.orchestration/` stores immutable run,

   verified long-lived preferences; update it only after a direct approved
   decision or implemented-and-verified lasting change, replace superseded
   statements instead of appending duplicates, and cite the owning decision or

   no durable evidence or decision links.

   evidence, packets, and historical `agent/` files into `.agent/` and

## Question 289: Guidance Position in 3D

Status: provisionally self-answered; user override remains open

Provisional selection: 1

Why it matters: a cue that merely floats on the phone screen can feel like a
webpage and may not tell the player which wall, anchor, object, or safe viewing
position matters. A cue that exists only in world space can disappear when its
target is off-camera or tracking is unavailable.

Question: Where should contextual guidance live relative to the physical scene?

1. Use a semantic hybrid. When a verified target pose is valid and visible,
   attach a restrained world-locked marker and short label to its safe cue
   socket. When the same target is off-camera, project its pose into one
   non-gameplay screen-edge direction cue; when no trustworthy pose exists,
   replace it with one screen-centered recovery instruction such as
   `Scan wall`, `Move back`, or `Re-place`. Bind all forms to one stable cue id
   and semantic action, preserve equivalent audio-intent, haptic, non-color,
   reduced-motion, and screen-reader output, avoid critical scene occlusion,
   and never let presentation advance gameplay.
2. Put every instruction in a permanent screen-bottom HUD, using no
   world-locked or offscreen directional cues.
3. Put every cue entirely inside the 3D world and show nothing when its target
   is outside the camera view, occluded, stale, or not yet anchored.
4. Draw free-floating arrows wherever they appear visually convenient each
   frame, without stable target ids, cue sockets, projection rules, or
   tracking-validity checks.

   context until the route is completely finished.

   entire route and reveals its collectible and ending before gameplay starts.
