## Verified Versus Proposed

Verified:

- Page 02 already targets a north-wall anchor and a wall-focus camera.
- Page 02 already has a breathing frame, glyph alignment, and portal opening.
- Page 05 already has a runner, three hazards, jump interactions, and a goal
  gate.
- Page 05 currently targets a center-floor tabletop presentation.
- Page 08 currently owns the eight-socket final portal.

Proposed, not implemented:

- Page 05 mounted inside a wall picture frame;
- a platforming world extending behind the canvas;
- a combined Page 02 and Page 05 interaction grammar;
- optional physical viewpoint inspection and its accessibility parity;
- direct continuity with Museum Multiverse's final level.

## Preserved Intentions

- The experience should feel fully spatial and 3D, not like a webpage inside a
  decorative frame.
- Placeholder visuals should eventually be replaced with deliberate art and
  models that support the frame-world illusion.
- Scene placement must account for wall orientation, frame pivot, scale,
  bounds, surface contact, player-relative readability, and occlusion.
- Product-specific presentation belongs in Lost Pages; reusable deterministic
  mechanics belong behind domain-service contracts.
- All eight routes should ultimately become substantial, replayable
  experiences rather than tiny interaction demos.
- The eventual skill plan must include frame, domain-service, 3D orientation,
  object-placement, gameplay, feedback, and validation capabilities.
