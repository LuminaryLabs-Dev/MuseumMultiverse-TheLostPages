# Idea: Picture-Frame Platformer

Status: evolving

## Current Synthesis

The strongest current concept combines:

- Page 02's wall-mounted breathing frame and portal setup;
- Page 05's miniature runner, hazards, jumping, and goal gate;
- the spatial fantasy of Museum Multiverse's final level, where a picture is a
  traversable world rather than a flat screen.

The proposed experience begins by detecting a wall and mounting an upright
frame. The canvas then opens into a layered miniature world with depth behind
and possibly in front of the picture plane. The player traverses a short
platforming route and reaches a portal or collectible.
