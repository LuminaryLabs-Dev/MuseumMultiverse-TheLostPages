## Batch 079

Questions 391-395 received no user selection. These choices define Page 05's
stable hero-frame composition, aperture proportion, initial canvas opening,

direct user override and grant no asset generation, runtime implementation,

### 391. Page 05 Impossible Frame Fragment Composition

Selection: build one original `Conservator's Impossible Portal` around a
dark-oak structural core and unobstructed nested-paper aperture. Balance a
paper-repaired aged-gilt upper-left cornice, left/lower brass catalog rail,
upper-right olive folio hinge and stitched spine, and lower-right blackened
iron conservation clamp and support shoe. Keep fragments fixed,
presentation-only, non-collidable, and outside the gameplay clearance
envelope. Any passing staged frame remains a bounded structural donor behind
this authored identity.

Rationale:

- One stable asymmetric silhouette makes the Page 05 landmark recognizable.
- Fixed fragments protect placement, gameplay visibility, and key-art parity.
- Original composition prevents an available donor from defining hero identity.

### 392. Page 05 Playable Aperture Proportion

Selection: author one fixed `3:2` landscape aperture with canonical width `W`
and height `2W/3`. Preserve JR, depth, cue-margin, frame-clearance, and camera
rules relative to that aperture. Wall and tabletop hosts may choose only one
validated uniform root scale; no host or device may reshape or reflow course
coordinates. Runtime, key art, standard/seated views, accessibility, and
physical proof share the ratio.

Rationale:

- `3:2` preserves useful horizontal travel without the height loss of `16:9`.
- One immutable coordinate shape keeps semantic and rendered proof comparable.
- Failing an unfit placement is safer than silently changing gameplay.

### 393. Page 05 Canvas-to-Course Opening

Selection: after placement persists and exact content passes preflight, hold a
closed prepared canvas with contextual `Open Lost Page`. Run one fixed-camera,
skippable 1.8-second presentation that releases at most four pre-seamed leaves,
separates shallow paper layers, folds the lower leaf into a safe first landing,
places canonical JR on the start perch, and hands off to Plan. Perform no I/O,
decode, compile, semantic mutation, progress grant, or camera movement during
the reveal. Skipped, reduced-motion, static, low-tier, described, and
interrupted forms converge on an exact prepared or settled state.

Rationale:

- Explicit readiness prevents animation from hiding loading or failure.
- Leaf-to-course continuity makes the picture-frame fantasy immediately clear.
- Exact fallback states avoid half-open restore and accessibility divergence.

### 394. Page 05 Milestone Frame Reactions

Selection: use one bounded `Conservation Register`. The first checkpoint
advances a brass tab and olive stitch; the second closes the clamp one shallow
step and connects a repaired gold dovetail line; portal readiness pulses only
the nested-paper inner frame and resolves four corner marks; durable completion
joins the inlays into a repaired border and reveals the final Page 05 label.
All reactions remain local, deterministic, under 700 milliseconds,
presentation-only, and incapable of moving the frame root, silhouette,
aperture, wall contact, gameplay, or progression.

Rationale:

- Fragment-specific states communicate progress without a generic glow.
- Stable root and silhouette prevent authored motion from resembling drift.
- Persist-first presentation keeps renderer acknowledgment outside authority.

### 395. Page 05 Hero-Frame Material Finish

Selection: use one restrained `Handled Conservation Object` finish with matte
open-grain dark oak, muted worn gilt and visible paper repair, oxidized brass,
woven olive book cloth, waxed blackened iron, and warm rag paper. Bind them
through the shared semantic-ink, authored-substrate, and mask-safe-patina
layers, coherent physical microtexture scale, restrained saturation, repeated
repair seams, authored frame-local lighting, clamped neutral ambience,
traceable source lineage, and accessibility-first tier reductions.

Rationale:

- Tactile differences preserve the frame's mismatched-era identity.
- Repeated repair language and scale unify the fragments as one handled object.
- Bounded materials preserve readability and mobile parity without flattening
  the frame or depending on dynamic photorealism.
