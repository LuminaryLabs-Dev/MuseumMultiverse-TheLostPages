# Picture-Frame Platformer Decision Ledger

## Batch 083

Questions 411-415 received no user selection. These choices define the first
critical surface pass, its bay-specific role map, lighting proof, performance
tier ladder, and consumer handoff. They remain open to direct user override and
grant no asset generation, runtime implementation, rights-sensitive reuse,
placeholder retirement, promotion, push, or deployment authority.

### 411. First Surface Pass

Selection: begin with one `Critical Readability Surface Grammar`. Give final
semantic material identities and non-color treatments to JR, safe contacts,
fold and tear cues, bridge, stair, ramp, recovery, and handoff under neutral
authored lighting before decorative frame finish, backing, atmosphere,
microdetail, effects, or key art.

Rationale:

- The current generic material and glow do not prove distinct gameplay roles.
- Critical readability must survive without decorative support.
- Later visual work consumes a stable semantic surface foundation.

### 412. Walking-Bay Critical Surface Map

Selection: use one `Role-Locked Conservation Map`. Preserve JR's stable palette
and apply shared safe, hazard, recovery, and handoff grammars over bounded rag
paper, olive cloth, book-spine, blackened-iron, and repaired-gilt treatments.
Authored light/dark variants, textures, silhouettes, labels, and independent
semantics carry meaning beyond color or substrate.

Rationale:

- Mixed conservation materials retain Page 05 identity without fragmenting the
  learned gameplay language.
- Natural substrate cannot silently decide whether a surface is safe or harmful.
- Explicit role bindings remain testable across tiers and accessibility forms.

### 413. Walking-Bay Readability Light Proof

Selection: gate critical surfaces with one `Pinned Readability Light Matrix`.
Serialize exact effect-free neutral, low-ambient, bright-frontal, hard-raking,
and fixed warm/cool synthetic-background fixtures across canonical phone,
physical-view, static, high-contrast, and low-tier crops. Live room pixels and
auto-exposure cannot select semantic meaning.

Rationale:

- Deterministic stress fixtures expose weak contrast before atmosphere hides it.
- Exact cameras and tones make surface evidence reproducible.
- Non-color carriers must survive the full accepted viewing range.

### 414. Walking-Bay Surface Tier Package

Selection: use one `Semantic-Constant Surface Ladder`. Preserve semantic ids,
masks, critical edges and marks, silhouettes, labels, contact geometry, and
independent collision across C1/C2/C3 while reducing only substrate resolution,
normal/roughness detail, patina, reflection, and decorative shadow response.
Accessibility remains orthogonal; tier replacement is checkpoint-only with
complete parity proof and rollback.

Rationale:

- Performance class cannot change gameplay language.
- Preauthored tiers are safer than runtime loss of critical art.
- Missing valid C1 blocks reveal instead of silently removing cues.

### 415. Critical-Surface-to-Decoration Handoff

Selection: require one hash-bound `Critical Surface Acceptance Packet`. Pin
critical maps, bindings, sources, masks, lighting evidence, tier manifests,
accessibility variants, views, performance, fallbacks, risks, and
producer/consumer verdicts. Open frame finish, backing/dressing,
atmosphere/motion/effects, microdetail, and key art as separate bounded
consumers with scoped invalidation.

Rationale:

- Downstream work consumes exact accepted critical inputs.
- Dependency changes stale only affected decorative nodes.
- Decoration gains no critical-mask, gameplay, retirement, promotion, push, or
  deployment authority.
