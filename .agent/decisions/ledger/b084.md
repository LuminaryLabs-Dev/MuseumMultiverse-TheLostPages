# Picture-Frame Platformer Decision Ledger

## Batch 084

Questions 416-420 received no user selection. These choices define the first
decorative consumer, its focal hierarchy, visible repair network,
distance-detail ladder, and handoff to backing and dressing. They remain open
to direct user override and grant no asset generation, runtime implementation,
rights-sensitive reuse, critical-mask or gameplay change, placeholder
retirement, promotion, push, or deployment authority.

### 416. First Decorative Consumer

Selection: begin with one `Hero-Frame Cohesion Pass`. Finish the accepted oak,
repaired gilt, brass, olive folio, and blackened-iron frame fragments across
tiers, accessible static forms, phone Plan/Run views, and wall/tabletop hosts
while root, aperture, wall contact, opening, clearance, critical masks, camera,
and semantics remain fixed.

Rationale:

- Page 05 needs a recognizable mounted picture-frame object before its
  surroundings consume attention or budget.
- Full-object composition should govern atmosphere and microdetail.
- The current generic scene supplies no frame-specific cohesion proof.

### 417. Hero-Frame Focal Hierarchy

Selection: use one `Aperture-First Conservation Hierarchy`. Keep the aperture
and route primary in placement and Plan, dark oak as the stable outer object,
the repaired-gilt and blackened-iron diagonal as restrained identity, and
brass and olive fragments secondary. JR and the next cue outrank all fixed
frame detail during Run.

Rationale:

- The playable path must remain immediately legible inside an elaborate frame.
- A restrained diagonal gives the assembled object identity without uniform
  visual noise.
- Fixed non-color hierarchy survives lighting, tiers, static forms, and hosts.

### 418. Hero-Frame Repair Cohesion

Selection: use one `Four-Motif Visible Repair Network`. Bind warm-paper
dovetail keys, oxidized-brass pins and saddles, short olive stitch wraps, and
quiet blackened-iron support shoes to exact non-gameplay edges at coherent
scale, bounded count, and low focal weight.

Rationale:

- Visible conservation repairs unify different substrates without hiding
  source and adaptation history.
- A small motif family is recognizable across device tiers.
- Reserved gameplay marks cannot be mistaken for decorative repair.

### 419. Hero-Frame Detail Scale Budget

Selection: use one `Three-Distance Detail Ladder`. Preserve silhouette,
aperture, major fragments, and identity diagonal everywhere; simplify
`0.25J-1J` borders, repairs, and labels by tier; and confine sub-`0.25J`
microdetail to noncritical C3 masks while C2 merges and C1 removes it. Runtime
and key art use one accepted revision.

Rationale:

- Full-object identity matters before close-up texture.
- Authored tier reductions prevent shimmer and uncontrolled phone density.
- Key art should document the playable object rather than invent a richer one.

### 420. Hero-Frame Cohesion Handoff

Selection: require one hash-bound `Hero-Frame Cohesion Packet`. Pin the
prerequisite surface packet, exact frame and source revision, fixed spatial and
gameplay protections, focal/repair/detail contracts, tier/static parity, and
phone, physical, host, browser/human-view, performance, fallback, risk, and
producer/consumer proof. Unlock only the deterministic backplane and one
bounded dressing consumer through declared sockets, rear depth, masks, budgets,
and fallbacks.

Rationale:

- Backing and dressing need one exact accepted frame and attachment envelope.
- Two-sided evidence prevents a producer artifact from silently becoming a
  consumer assumption.
- Dependency changes stale affected work without granting broader authority.
