# Picture-Frame Platformer Decision Ledger

## Batch 072

Questions 356-360 received no user selection. These choices reaffirm Page 05's
relationship to the prior Museum Multiverse finale and define Page 03's hero
sketchbook, creature-platform forms, motion cues, and reward. They remain open
to direct user override and grant no asset generation, runtime implementation,
rights-sensitive reuse, push, or deployment authority.

### 356. Page 05 Museum Multiverse Finale Relationship

Selection: make Page 05 a clear spiritual successor. Preserve the fantasy of
JR entering and platforming inside a living picture frame, a readable head-on
course, and miniature depth, while authoring a new Lost Pages layered-paper
route, hazards, transformations, story purpose, art, and reward. Page 02
teaches the frame language and Page 08 later remixes a small learned subset.

Rationale:

- Earlier provisional Questions 019 and 218 already select the same
  original-content relationship.
- No accepted source evidence supports recreating the earlier route or assets.
- The structure keeps Page 05 distinctive while all eight routes retain one
  shared platformer foundation.

### 357. Page 03 Living Sketchbook Landmark Form

Selection: build one original cloth-bound conservation sketchbook on a shallow
tabletop cradle. Its deckled warm-paper spread integrates a stitched spine,
graphite construction, fixed embossed `Glide`, `Lift`, and `Loop` guides,
raised landing tabs, rust-orange thread, and a translucent palimpsest memory
window. The book, support contact, scale, paths, and semantic collision remain
fixed while creature art may lift into bounded paper relief.

Rationale:

- Current Page 03 is a generic paper card with three identical scribbles.
- A stable book-and-cradle silhouette expresses its story-native tabletop
  placement and mechanic.
- Original art avoids unsupported use of a real child's private material.

### 358. Page 03 Creature-Platform Forms

Selection: make `Glide` a long-backed horizontally ribbed pencil hound with a
broad paper saddle, `Lift` a vertically hatched wide-winged moth with a centered
landing tab, and `Loop` a dotted curled-tail salamander with a crescent paper
saddle. Stable silhouettes, labels, tracks, endpoints, arrival beats,
descriptions, and the shared safe double edge communicate roles without color.
Semantic landing volumes stay independent, and catching copies only a margin
impression.

Rationale:

- The current identical scribbles do not communicate three different motion
  roles or safe landing surfaces.
- Motion-predictive forms let players read the route before active traversal.
- Catching remains optional and cannot remove a required platform.

### 359. Page 03 Motion Readability

Selection: give every creature one faint complete graphite path, named endpoint
stamps, direction hatching, fixed phase ticks, short bounded live trace,
next-dwell ghost, and an approach-window saddle edge. Concise labels, optional
pencil beats, and screen-reader announcements derive from the authoritative
phase. Pause and restore retain that phase; reduced effects preserve static
timing meaning; presentation never changes motion or eligibility.

Rationale:

- Current moving scribbles provide no predictive timing language.
- Stable multimodal cues support anticipation rather than memorization or fast
  reaction alone.
- Renderer effects remain subordinate to deterministic gameplay state.

### 360. Page 03 Memory Sketch Form

Selection: bind display name `Memory Sketch` to stable id
`memory-sketch-fragment`. Use one original asymmetric deckled-paper and
translucent-vellum palimpsest shard whose three creature paths converge around
a missing-center doorway, with rust-orange stitching, graphite texture, Page
03 and slot-three marks, accessible label, three-stitch folio attachment, and
a simplified Page 08 imprint. Completion and reward receipts precede the
memory-window reveal; revisit is non-interactive and optional caught
impressions remain decorative.

Rationale:

- Current copy and storage name the reward but render no unique artifact.
- The form connects route, folio, and finale without collecting personal
  player content or copying a real child's material.
- Receipt-first reveal preserves reward ownership through interruption.
