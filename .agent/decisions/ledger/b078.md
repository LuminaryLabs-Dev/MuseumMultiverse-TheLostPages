# Picture-Frame Platformer Decision Ledger

## Batch 078

Questions 386-390 received no user selection. These choices define the
MMGDoc/staged-asset adaptation boundary, route-role assignment, authoring
source of truth, compound-model decomposition, and image-region derivation.
They remain open to direct user override and grant no asset generation,
runtime implementation, rights-sensitive reuse, push, or deployment authority.

### 386. MMGDoc Asset Adaptation Boundary

Selection: admit a cleared source only as a bounded gated visual donor after
provenance, rights, integrity, and route-fit acceptance. Normalize its spatial,
material, texture, LOD, and tier contract; bind it over independent authored
semantics; preserve source and transformation lineage, attribution, fallbacks,
package identity, and review evidence; and reject ambiguity. Signature
landmarks, mechanisms, rewards, critical cues, JR, and story-defining
silhouettes remain original Lost Pages work unless element-level reuse is
separately authorized.

Rationale:

- Local availability proves neither rights nor production suitability.
- A bounded donor can reduce support-art work without becoming gameplay truth.
- Original hero identity prevents staged assets from defining the experience.

### 387. Source Asset Route-Role Assignment

Selection: use one need-first route-role registry. An approved route recipe
emits a typed visual brief covering role, criticality, host, envelope,
material/motif family, readability, accessibility, budgets, tiers, rights, and
fallback. The gate accepts zero or one primary binding per candidate revision
and declares any shared use separately. Route, role, hashes, transformation,
rationale, rejection, and evidence remain pinned. No available asset may change
mechanics, landmarks, or story; an unsuitable inventory routes to an approved
procedural or custom replacement.

Rationale:

- Route needs should govern asset selection rather than the reverse.
- Explicit primary and shared bindings preserve distinct route identities.
- A legal rejection path prevents forced or opportunistic reuse.

### 388. Adapted Asset Authoring Source

Selection: preserve accepted original bytes and one repo-owned versioned
adaptation package containing the editable DCC source or declarative recipe,
pinned tools/plugins, normalization, mesh/material/texture/LOD/compression and
export rules, accessibility/fallback bindings, attribution, and output hashes.
Manual sculpting or paint work becomes an explicit source revision and
reproducible handoff. Every export creates a new candidate and invokes existing
dependency-scoped invalidation while the accepted last-known-good package stays
untouched until atomic promotion.

Rationale:

- Export-only binaries cannot explain or reproduce artistic and technical
  adaptation.
- Explicit manual-edit lineage avoids false claims of determinism.
- Immutable candidates and last-known-good retention make revisions auditable
  and rollback-safe.

### 389. Compound Asset Decomposition

Selection: preserve the original compound scene and map selected source parts
non-destructively into stable visual modules. Each output pins its route role,
native adapter, pivot, bounds, materials, tiers, budget, fallback, and source
paths/hashes; it may combine selected parts, quarantine unused parts, and
deduplicate shared buffers. Ambiguous hierarchy, hidden dependencies,
unsupported features, and unexplained extras fail closed. Independently
authored semantic modules retain collision, sockets, interaction, and topology.

Rationale:

- Whole-file loading carries unused cost and obscures visual roles.
- Source hierarchy and names are unreliable gameplay contracts.
- A traceable decomposition map supports optimization without losing lineage.

### 390. Image and Texture Donor Extraction

Selection: preserve each original image and use an explicit region-level
derivative map for every crop, mask, channel, vector trace, or painted layer.
Pin source and authored-diff identity, rights, color/alpha, route role, physical
material or effect scale, UV/channels, palette treatment, atlas/mip/compression,
tiers, fallback, attribution, and output hashes. Manual restoration or
paint-over creates a new authored revision. Independent approved cue carriers
own critical outlines, symbols, state masks, and text; source pixels and shader
output never define gameplay.

Rationale:

- Whole-image reuse preserves unwanted composition and lacks material scale.
- Region-level lineage keeps derivatives reproducible and rights-auditable.
- Separate cue carriers preserve meaning across compression, tiers, color
  perception, and renderer failure.
