## Batch 001

Questions 001-005 received no user reply. Per the interview rule, the following
working selections were derived from `.agent/` evidence and current source.

### 1. Experience Scope

Selection: Page 02 teaches the frame; Page 05 delivers the full platformer.

Rationale:

- Page 02 already owns wall placement, glyph alignment, and portal opening.
- Page 05 already owns the runner, jumping, hazards, and goal gate.
- Combining their existing responsibilities produces a clear tutorial-to-payoff
  arc without forcing the same frame structure onto all eight pages.

### 2. Player Control

Selection: simple avatar input plus physical movement for perspective and hidden
paths.

Rationale:

- Page 05 already describes pointer-driven jumping.
- Physical viewpoint movement gives AR a meaningful role without making body
  motion the sole accessibility gate.
- The hybrid supports hero controls on the first screen while preserving spatial
  discovery.

### 3. Frame Depth

Selection: most geometry stays behind the canvas; restrained effects or small
objects may cross the frame.

Rationale:

- The frame remains readable as a portal mounted to a detected wall.
- Contained depth reduces unsafe room-scale protrusions and surface-contact
  ambiguity.
- Small crossings still sell the illusion that the world is genuinely spatial.

### 4. Core Challenge

Selection: inspect the scene, alter the route, then traverse it.

Rationale:

- The long-term experience goal requires observe, choose, act, receive feedback,
  adapt, and complete.
- A timing-only course would preserve the current small-demo limitation.
- Route state creates useful deterministic domain-service ownership.

### 5. Replacement Art

Selection: selected museum assets, custom hero pieces, and deterministic
procedural support scenery.

Rationale:

- A frame model and multiple frame, painting, and portal textures are staged in
  `public/assets/mmgdoc/`.
- Their availability does not prove mesh, collision, visual, or attribution
  suitability, so an existing-assets-only direction is premature.
- Custom hero pieces establish Lost Pages identity; procedural support can reach
  the intended object density without hundreds of manual entries.
