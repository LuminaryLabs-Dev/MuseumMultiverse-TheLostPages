# Picture-Frame Platformer Decision Ledger

## Batch 085

Questions 421-425 received no user selection. These choices define the first
structural backplane layer, rear-depth ownership, first dressing family,
negative-space composition, and handoff to atmosphere and landmark feedback.
They remain open to direct user override and grant no asset generation, runtime
implementation, rights-sensitive reuse, semantic or placement change,
placeholder retirement, promotion, push, or deployment authority.

### 421. First Backplane Layer

Selection: begin with one `Clearance-First Structural Backplane Pass`. Generate
only a shallow neutral rear panel, perimeter rails, and minimum cross-braces
around the pinned backing root, frame sockets, rear-depth envelope, keepouts,
visibility masks, wall-contact separation, and tabletop fallback before
dressing, atmosphere, or effects.

Rationale:

- Structure should prove clearance and deterministic regeneration before
  decoration obscures it.
- The current generic scene has no backing hierarchy.
- Non-traversable support cannot become hidden gameplay or placement authority.

### 422. Backplane Depth Zoning

Selection: use one `Four-Zone Rear-Depth Contract`. Order `G0` protected
gameplay/visibility void, `D1` shallow dressing relief, `S2` structural
backplane, and `P3` physical-placement isolation from the player-facing front
toward the independent wall-contact plane and support hull.

Rationale:

- Typed planes prevent visual content from drifting into gameplay clearance.
- Dressing, structure, and physical placement retain separate ownership.
- Fixed zones remain comparable across tiers, views, and hosts.

### 423. First Dressing Family

Selection: begin with one `Quiet Paper-and-Binding Study Set`. Bind shallow
folio offcuts, closed book-spine clusters, paper swatches, thread sample cards,
and blank-edged catalog tabs only to declared `D1` dressing sockets outside
critical masks. Keep them low-contrast, motionless, noninteractive,
non-traversable, and non-instructional.

Rationale:

- Paper, books, and bindings reinforce the first walking bay's established
  material identity.
- A closed family avoids an incoherent all-era asset sampler.
- Quiet dressing cannot masquerade as route, hazard, cue, or interaction.

### 424. Dressing Composition Map

Selection: use one `Protected Negative-Space Pocket Map`. Project all critical
gameplay, aperture, frame, repair, accessibility, and accepted-view masks and
derive up to three stable named dressing pockets only from their shared
complement. Bind one low-relief cluster per pocket without tracing the route.

Rationale:

- Cross-view negative space protects the full lower-left to upper-right route.
- Stable pockets make tier removal and late streaming deterministic.
- Empty fallback preserves layout, depth, visibility, and gameplay meaning.

### 425. Backing-to-Atmosphere Handoff

Selection: require one hash-bound `Backplane and Dressing Acceptance Packet`.
Pin the prerequisite frame packet, exact structure, depth zones, sockets,
inventories, sources, pockets, tiers/static forms, streaming/fallback behavior,
regeneration, cross-host proof, performance, risks, and producer/consumer
verdicts. Open only one bounded atmosphere and landmark-feedback consumer with
declared lighting, shadow, contrast, motion, particle, multimodal,
accessibility, and device budgets.

Rationale:

- Atmosphere must consume one reproducible backing and dressing revision.
- Two-sided proof catches occlusion, shadow, streaming, and tier failures
  before effects can hide them.
- Dependency changes stale affected work without granting broader authority.
